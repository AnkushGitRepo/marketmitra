# Changelog

All notable changes to MarketMitra are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Markets now has a "Bullion & ETFs" view with estimated Indian prices for gold (24K and 22K), silver and platinum, a gold-to-silver ratio, an include-3%-GST toggle, and a sortable table of NSE gold and silver ETFs. It says plainly that the prices are estimates and shows the import duty used and when it was last confirmed. (#450)

- A public `GET /api/commodities` endpoint with estimated Indian prices for gold 24K and 22K (per 10 g), silver (per kg) and platinum (per gram), the gold/silver ratio and the NSE gold and silver ETFs. The prices are the international price converted to rupees with India's 15% import duty, so they track the Indian rate closely for gold but are not the official IBJA or MCX rate. (#448)

- The Portfolio value chart now has a "vs Nifty 50" view that plots your portfolio and the Nifty 50 as percent change over the same range, with the difference in percentage points, and a "Range" view that shades a statistical range ahead from the portfolio's own past volatility. The range is not a forecast and says so. (#433)

- Click a holding on the Portfolio page to open a side panel with its entry date, your thesis, the decisions you recorded on it and its own news, with a link to the full stock page. (#432)

- Each holding on the Portfolio page now shows a small 52-week range bar under its price and a "Near 52W high" or "Near 52W low" badge when the price is within 5% of either end. (#431)
- News for your holdings on the Portfolio page now shows which holding each story touches and its weight in your book, a Bullish / Bearish / Neutral tag with a High impact, Material or Routine level worked out from the story's own sentiment score and the position size, and filters for High impact, Bullish, Bearish and by stock. (#430)
- Mitra can now read your conviction board, decision journal, cash and tax view, so you can ask what is on your board, what you thought when you bought a stock, how much cash you hold or which positions turn long term soon. These are read-only: Mitra describes the numbers and does not tell you to buy or sell, and the tax answers say they are estimates, not tax advice. ([#419](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/419))
- A Tax & Harvesting page (Portfolio, then Tax). Give each holding its purchase date and it shows whether the position is short or long term and how many days remain until it passes 12 months, the unrealised gain by term, an estimate of the tax on gains you have already booked this year and the extra if everything were sold today, the days left to 31 March, and which positions are at a loss with what a realised loss would save against your booked gains. It is an estimate, not tax advice: rates are short term 20% and long term 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh a year, and a holding without a date is left out rather than guessed. ([#414](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/414))
- A Cash & Liquid Funds page (Portfolio, then Cash) with a what-if rebalance. Record the cash and liquid funds you hold outside your stocks and see them as their own slice in the allocation (with a switch), then try changes before making them: put money into a stock or fund, or trim a holding, and see the top sector, top three holdings, largest position and cash share before and after. Nothing is saved or traded, and cash is part of "Export my data". ([#413](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/413))
- A decision journal. Whenever you add, change or remove a position (from the Portfolio form, a file import or Mitra), it saves what you knew at that moment: the price, P/E and ROCE, the note you had written about the stock, which guardrail alerts were active and an optional one-line reason. Each entry then shows what happened since, and an exit has a box for what you got right or wrong. The entries for a stock sit in a new My journal tab on its stock page (separate from the analyst's Agent journal), in an Investment thesis & entry snapshot card with a thesis and exit-trigger note you can edit. You can edit your own reason, delete any entry, or delete the whole journal from Settings under Data & Privacy, and it is included in "Export my data". ([#412](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/412))
- A conviction board on the Markets page: add a stock, tag how sure you are (High, Tracking, or Waiting for something), set a target entry price and link the note that holds your thesis. Setting a target creates a price alert for you, and the board shows how far each stock is from its target. ([#411](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/411))
- Mitra now remembers what you tell it and uses it in later chats: say that you are a conservative investor with a 12 year horizon who avoids tobacco and it saves each fact (with a chip in the reply and a one-click Undo), and every new chat starts from them. It learns only from your own messages (never from files, web pages or notes), never keeps identity, bank or login details, and Settings under AI & Models lists everything it remembers with Delete, Add, Forget everything and an on/off switch. Memories are part of "Export my data". ([#402](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/402))
- Mitra can search the web and read a page when a question needs fresh facts (a recent result, ruling or news item), with up to three searches and two pages per reply, sources and dates named in the answer, in the Mitra page and the chat drawer. It only searches public names, opens only addresses from its own search results or ones you typed, is switched off while a file is attached, and has a Web search switch in Settings under AI & Models. ([#401](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/401))
- Mitra now works with your notes properly. Asking "list my notes" shows every note in an interactive card (Open and Ask Mitra buttons, kept in plain text when Rich replies is off) instead of naming one of three, new `list_notes` and `get_note` tools let it list and read any note in full, and your notes are read on every question: an index of them, the passages closest to what you asked, and an excerpt of any note about a stock you just named, so a saved research report informs the answer without you asking. Works in the Mitra page and the chat drawer, and `/notes?note=<id>` opens a note. ([#400](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/400))
- Notes can be exported as Word (.docx), Markdown or plain text as well as PDF, and a note's tickers can be added and removed right under its title; a ticker links the note to that stock for Mitra and the stock page. ([#396](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/396))
- The Compare tab on Research now writes a seven-chapter head-to-head report instead of three generic blocks: category leaders (scale, profitability and ROCE, balance-sheet safety, growth momentum), one combined chart of every company's revenue, operating profit and growth from a common base year with a dashed, labelled projection, a head-to-head matrix with the best figure of each row marked plus sector-specific measures found by search (ARPU and subscriber share for telecom, NIM and GNPA for banks; "not found" where no source gives one), a profile and edge per company, company-specific issues tagged CRITICAL, MODERATE or NORMAL, valuation against growth, and the questions that separate the companies. It streams chapter by chapter, has the same jump bar and PDF, Word, Markdown and text export, and one-click rival presets (Jio vs Airtel vs Vi, HDFC Bank vs ICICI vs SBI, TCS vs Infosys vs HCLTech, HAL vs BEL vs Mazagon Dock). ([#394](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/394))
- Tables on Research no longer push their last column off the card (headers and company names wrap), and the page leaves room under the last block so the chat button never covers a chart. ([#394](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/394))
- The Portfolio tab on Research now writes a seven-chapter forensic review of your holdings instead of repeating the Portfolio page: what carried the result and what dragged it (with a health score and how much of the gain two stocks carry), every holding below cost tagged CRITICAL, MODERATE or NORMAL with why it fell (the model searches the web for the worst holdings and the largest positions), the economic drivers behind the book beyond sector labels, a weighted quality and valuation scorecard, a one-volatility twelve-month range worked out from each holding's own price swings (a range of how far the book has tended to move, not a forecast), a ranked risk register and a stock-by-stock checklist for the next review. It streams chapter by chapter and has the same jump bar and PDF, Word, Markdown and text export as the other reports. ([#390](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/390))
- The sector report is less of a wall of text: the value chain is drawn as a row of cards with margin and moat tags, the India-versus-global comparison as a table (a figure that was not found says so), the outlook, policy and risk chapters open with green, amber and red callout cards, and the ten-question checklist is a numbered two-column card grid. They appear in the page, the PDF and the Word, Markdown and text files, and only when the written chapters support them. ([#386](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/386))
- The Theme tab on Research now writes a nine-chapter sector report instead of a one-paragraph brief: sector overview and value chain, a financial scorecard with a labelled growth chart, the top listed Indian companies with who-leads-on-what, unlisted innovators and the IPO pipeline, global benchmarks, outlook and catalysts, policy and regulation, litigation and governance risks, and a ten-question investor checklist. The figures come from stored data, the chapters stream in as they are written, the model searches the web for the rest, and it has the same jump bar and PDF, Word, Markdown and text export as the company report. ([#380](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/380))
- The Theme tab on Research has a predictive picker: type "def", "rail" or "pharma" and it suggests matching themes, sectors and industries from over 40 curated Indian market themes and the screener's own data, each with its number of listed stocks and its largest tickers. Picking one, or pressing Enter, generates the report. It replaces the six fixed buttons. ([#378](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/378))
- A sticky bar of chapter links now sits at the top of the company report (Overview, Financials, Peers, Sector, Regulatory & legal, Management, Valuation, Risks), so you can jump straight to a chapter. It appears as soon as the figures do, follows you as you scroll by highlighting the chapter you are reading, and on a phone scrolls sideways. Links work with the keyboard and move focus to the chapter; smooth scrolling is turned off if you have asked your system for reduced motion. ([#361](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/361))
- The company report can be exported in four formats from one "Export report" menu (enabled once every chapter has finished): a PDF with a cover page (letterhead, headline figures, contents and a disclaimer), a faint watermark and a running footer on every page, tables that fit the page and no browser date or address printed on it; a Word document with real headings (so Word's navigation pane works), tables for the figures and "Page X of Y"; a Markdown file with front matter (ticker, date, market cap, P/E) and pipe tables; and a plain-text file with aligned tables. The PDF is made through your browser's print dialog (choose Save as PDF), so its contents list has no page numbers; Word and text files show a chart as the table of figures behind it. ([#372](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/372))
- The Research page now shows the full company report. Enter a company on the Company tab and the figures appear within a couple of seconds (the sales, margin and cash-flow charts, net debt, peers, P/E history, ownership and promoter pledge, exchange announcements, and a bear/base/bull range drawn from the company's own history), with each chapter's text filling in as it is written ("Writing…" with a progress line). The nine chapters are business and thesis, financials and cash flow, peers, sector, regulatory and policy, litigation and governance, management, valuation, and a rated risk register. Each chapter that searched the web lists what it searched and the sources it used, a chapter that fails says so and keeps its figures, and Save to Notes and Export PDF work on the whole report once it is done. The Theme, Portfolio and Compare tabs are unchanged. ([#360](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/360))
- New API endpoint `POST /api/research/report`: a nine-chapter research report on one listed company, streamed as it is written. Cards, charts and tables (five years of sales, margins and cash flow, net debt, peers, P/E history, ownership, exchange announcements) are computed from stored data and arrive first; the written chapters (business and thesis, financials, peers, sector, regulatory and policy, litigation and governance, management, valuation with bear/base/bull ranges drawn from the company's own history, and a rated risk register) follow as each finishes. Chapters that need current facts search the web before they say anything cannot be found, with Gemini, Claude and OpenAI keys using their own search and every other provider using the app's built-in search, and each chapter lists what was searched and the sources used. It is neutral analysis only (no buy/sell view or price target) and a full report takes one to three minutes on your own AI key. The Research page does not show it yet. ([#360](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/360))
- Research briefs can be saved and printed: a "Save to Notes" button on the report files it as a note (named after what you researched and the date, with its tickers, tables included) and links to it, and "Export PDF" prints just the report, without the sidebar, through your browser's print dialog. The Notes page is now a two-pane vault: your notes on the left with filters (All, Saved research, Stock notes, Sector & general) that show how many each holds, and a search over titles, text and tickers; the chosen note on the right, formatted, with Export PDF, "Ask Mitra about this note", Edit and Delete (which asks first). Each note shows whether it was saved from Research or written by you. Saved research notes can be up to 100,000 characters (your own notes stay at 4,000) and are searched by Mitra like any note. `POST /api/notes` accepts `source` and `tickers`. ([#355](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/issues/355))
- Research briefs are now visual reports instead of a wall of text. Each one opens with summary cards, then charts and a sortable table suited to what you researched: sales and operating profit by year with a peer table for a company, the sector's largest companies and their growth for a theme, a sector donut and profit-and-loss by position for your portfolio, and a side-by-side table (best figure in each column in green) with a bar chart per metric for a comparison. Below them come the short written summary and three cards for catalysts, risks and open questions. Every number in the cards, charts and tables comes straight from the stored figures, not from the AI, and a company with no history simply leaves that chart out. If the AI does not answer in the expected format, you still get the figures and its text. `POST /api/research` now also returns `summary` and `blocks`; `content` is still markdown and now includes the tables.
