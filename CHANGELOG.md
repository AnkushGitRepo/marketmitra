# Changelog

All notable changes to MarketMitra. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org); entries are generated by `npm run release` from Conventional Commits.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-11

### Features

- a high daily AI cap for analysts on a local or self-hosted instance ([#143](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/pull/143))
- honest queue states, Analyze now and Retry for analysts waiting on a dossier ([#141](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/pull/141))
- thesis invalidation criteria checked every evening, with a journal entry and alert on breach ([#137](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/pull/137))
- morning dispatch of analyst journals by email and in-app at 08:30 IST ([#136](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/pull/136))
- chat with a stock analyst or the Root Agent from the Analyst Desk ([#135](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/pull/135))
- Analyst Desk UI with a Root Agent deck, honest card states, filters and a slide-over dossier ([#134](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/pull/134))
- stock analysts - a persistent AI analyst per holding with a dossier and journal ([#132](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/pull/132))
- structured Mitra briefs for the dashboard, markets, news and IPO widgets ([#126](https://github.com/AnkushGitRepo/marketmitra/pull/126))
